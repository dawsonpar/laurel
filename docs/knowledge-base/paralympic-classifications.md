## Why this matters

Paralympic sports group athletes by **classification**, not just by event. A 100m race in Para Athletics has dozens of separate classes (T11 visually impaired, T54 wheelchair, F56 seated throws, etc.). New viewers find this opaque, which is exactly the pain Laurel addresses.

When this page is filled in, it will include:

- The high-level taxonomy (sport class prefixes: T = track, F = field, S = swimming, etc.)
- The specific classes used in each MVP Paralympic sport
- A plain-English explanation of what each class means and why it exists
- How to read a result (eg "100m T11 — what does T11 tell you about this race?")
